## What it is

A **Curve-shape DLMM position with very tight bins (3-5 bins)** placed
at the current price during a memecoin's first bullish leg on high
volume. The position lives for **under 5 minutes**. You harvest the
massive fee flow from micro-rebounds on the 15-second timeframe, then
exit before the move dies.

Named "heart attack" because:
- Your entire position flips from one token to the other in 1-2 candles
- A wrong entry = instant heavy loss (tight range = no buffer)
- Watching it live = actual heart attack territory

---

## The playbook (step by step)

1. **SPOT** a token with a big pump on REAL volume (100K-1M+ in the
   first hour — not wash, not farmed)
2. **WAIT** for the first small drawback/pullback after the initial
   blast (don't top-blast into the first candle)
3. **OPEN** a Curve-shape position, 3-5 bins, centered at current price
   (Curve = liquidity concentrated at the active bin)
4. **RIDE** the micro-rebounds on the 15-second timeframe — every
   bounce through your 3-5 bins pays fees at max density
5. **EXIT FAST** — fees + minimal price movement = the win. Don't wait
   for the trend. Don't get greedy. Minutes, not hours.

---

## Why it works (the mechanics)

1. **No slippage:** tight bins around the active price = traders get
   the best execution anywhere. They *prefer* your pool even with
   higher fees.
2. **Fee priority:** your pool gets picked first for every swap,
   so you accumulate fees at maximum velocity.
3. **Curve shape:** liquidity concentrated at the active bin =
   every trade pays you, not the liquidity spread across 1000 bins.
4. **Volume = everything:** 100K-1M+ volume in an hour means your
   3-5 bins process the entire flow of the launch.

## The risks (stated honestly by everyone)

1. **Out of range fast = IL is brutal.** A tight range flips your
   entire position to one token within 1-2 candles. If the price
   dumps through, you hold a bag at the worst price.
2. **LP Army Academy official warning:** *"Heart Attack strategies
   come with extremely high risk and are more like gambling.
   Not recommended to attempt."*
3. **The prerequisite kills most people:** molu requires "real
   execution experience. Auto Solflare, hotkeys, muscle memory
   on the interface, all of that has to be second nature before
   you touch this setup with real size."
4. **Timing = the whole game.** "If you time it well and avoid
   greed, you can still make a good profit." Greed is the killer.

## Key lessons from practitioners

- **Enter EARLY** in high-volume positions: big pump → small
  drawback → open position → pump continues
- **High volume is necessary**: aim for 100K-1M minimum in the hour
- **NEVER hold bags** in unreliable coins ("my biggest losses have
  come from this" — molu)
- **Don't get greedy**: exit on the first target, not the trend
- **The signal to enter**: big pump + small drawback + still elevated
  volume = the micro-rebound window
- **The signal to exit**: fees collected + first sign of the rebound
  dying = close, don't wait
