# @linuxz69 — 30-Day Original-Post Plan (5M Impressions Path)

**Rule corrected:** Ads revenue sharing counts impressions on ORIGINAL posts and quotes — replies don't count. So the strategy: keep replying for GROWTH (followers, credibility), but 2–3 original posts/day for the MONETIZATION counter.

## The math
- Goal: 5M impressions in a rolling 3 months ≈ 1.67M/month ≈ 55K/day
- Realistic mix per day: 2–3 originals (500–5K views each) + 1 weekly thread (5–50K) + 5–10 quote-tweets (1–10K each) + viral lottery ticket
- Reply reach does NOT count — but replies convert readers into followers, and followers multiply every original's baseline reach

## Your voice rules (from reply-style.md)
- First line = hook. No preamble.
- Wallet lens: price is the print, flow is the signal
- Short declaratives. Periods. One-line laws.
- One emoji max, or none
- Never shill a bag, never give token calls

## The 7 Content Formats (rotate)

### F1 — The Daily Ledger (morning, ~9AM CT)
Live data post: what the wallets did overnight.
Template:
> Solana morning ledger [date]:
> $2.4B traded, 31M transactions. Top launch: [X] at [Y] mcap, [Z]h old.
> Spider check: [spider funder] funded [n] top-holders this week.
> The chain never sleeps. Neither does the machine.

### F2 — The Print vs Signal (reactive, when a headline drops)
Take trending news, apply the wallet lens.
Template:
> $[NUMBER] is the print. It is not the signal. The check is [flow metric]. [One-line law.]

### F3 — Rug-Check Receipts (2x/week)
Post a real scan result (from insider_radar.py).
Template:
> Rug-checked [n] trending launches today:
> • [n] mint revoked ✅
> • [n] LP locked ✅
> • [n] with clustered insider wallets ⚠️
> The contract can't rug you. The holders can.

### F4 — The Spider Thread (weekly, Sunday — your weapon)
The AgmLJBMD series. Each week: +1 token confirmed, screenshot the trace.
Structure: 4–7 posts, every post one fact. Final post = the law.

### F5 — Position Diary (2x/week)
Your real LP numbers. Transparency = trust = followers.
Template:
> Day [n] LP diary:
> Range [X]–[Y], state [WAITING/IN RANGE]
> Fees: $[Z] | IL: $[W]
> Lesson: [one sentence]

### F6 — The Contrarian Stat (2x/week)
One counter-intuitive number with a law.
Templates:
> [Big number] in [period]. Divide by [real factor] and it becomes [honest number]. Headlines don't divide.
> Everyone watches [metric]. The wallet watches [better metric]. The difference is [law].

### F7 — Quote-Tweet Sniper (1–3x/day, counts as original!)
Quote big accounts' posts with the wallet lens. These COUNT toward impressions and pull their audience.
Template: take a Watcher.Guru/Cointelegraph headline → 1-2 line wallet take.

## Rules of discipline
1. **Never miss the Daily Ledger.** Consistency trains both the algorithm and the audience.

3. **Every original ends with a law.** Laws get quote-posted.
4. **The spider series is the moat.** Nobody else has this data. Nobody else can fake it.
5. **Measure weekly, adjust monthly.** The tracker comes later.
