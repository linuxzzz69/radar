# Crypto X Reply Style Guide (Witty / Bold)

Goal: replies that get likes, reposts, and profile clicks → follower growth.

## Your voice (@linuxz69)
Brand: on-chain analyst, reads wallets not headlines, plain English, no token calls.
Signature moves already working:
- Take a headline post and reframe it with the data lens: "$86,000 is the print. It is not the signal."
- Short declarative sentences. Periods over commas.
- End with a one-line law: "Price without the wallet is a headline."
Every reply must sound like the same person writing this. Skeptical, calm, specific.

## Rules
1. **First line = hook.** No "Great post!" — open with a take, a number, or a twist.
2. **Add, don't agree.** Every reply must add info, a counterpoint, or a punchline. Pure agreement gets zero reach.
3. **Crypto-native language.** Terms like degen, ape, paper hands, liquidity, alpha, rug, cope, NGMI, wagmi, "numba go up" — used sparingly and correctly.
4. **Keep it short.** 1–3 sentences. 280 chars is fine; under 140 is ideal.
5. **Specific beats vague.** Use numbers, tickers ($BTC, $ETH, $SOL), dates, mechanics (halving, ETF flows, funding rates).
6. **Never shill your own bag** in replies unless asked. Looks desperate, kills credibility.
7. **No financial advice vibes.** Wit, not price predictions you can be quoted on.
8. **Match the reply to the account size.** Big accounts (100k+): bold/quotable. Mid accounts: insightful + playful. Small accounts: genuinely conversational.
9. **Timing:** reply within 15–30 min of the post going up for best reach.
10. **One emoji max.** Or none.

## Reply formulas
- **Contrarian:** "Everyone says X. The data says Y." / "This aged poorly and it's only been 3 hours."
- **Add alpha:** take their point one step further with a fact/metric.
- **Punchline:** short joke with a truth inside it.
- **Story flex:** "I did X in 2021. Here's what actually happened:" (one line).

## Anti-patterns
- "Great thread 🔥" / "This!" / "+1"
- Wall-of-text lectures
- Arguing with rage-bait (engagement farming that reads desperate)
- Replying to every post by the same account in 5 minutes (looks bot-like)
- **Em-dashes (—) in any drafted text** — they read as AI-generated.
  Use periods, commas, or line breaks instead. Applies to replies,
  tweets, threads, DMs, letters: everything.
